GAME CONTENT / CONTENU DU JEU
-----------------------------
LifeLegend contains NO game file, no player database and no club crest. Player names, crests and competition
logos are read at runtime from the user's own copy of eFootball PES 2021 (and patches such as Football Life)
and stay on the user's computer.
LifeLegend ne contient aucun fichier du jeu : noms des joueurs, écussons et logos sont lus sur l'ordinateur du joueur,
dans son propre jeu, et y restent.

eFootball PES 2021 is a trademark of Konami Digital Entertainment. LifeLegend is an unofficial fan-made mod,
not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami, Football Life, any club, league, federation or player.


REAL PEOPLE / VRAIES PERSONNES
------------------------------
LifeLegend is fiction. Real players, coaches and clubs appear because they are in the user's game, but every
quote, post, reaction or email attributed to them is written by an AI and is NOT their words. Do not present
these texts as real or attribute them to the people concerned outside LifeLegend.
LifeLegend est une fiction : tout ce que les vraies personnes y disent ou font est écrit par l'IA, ce ne sont pas
leurs propos. Ne présentez jamais ces textes comme vrais et ne les leur attribuez pas hors de LifeLegend.
